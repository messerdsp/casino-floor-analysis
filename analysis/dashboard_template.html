4 · Availability: is the machine broken too often?

Availability = available hours ÷ scheduled hours (23 h on Mar 8). Lost play is valued at each down machine's own expected win for that hour of day.

5 · Leak: is any machine paying out more than its game math allows?

Two independent tests per machine. (1) Payout: house win vs. theo in volatility-scaled standard deviations. (2) Hit rate: share of spins that pay anything vs. the published hit probability (a binomial test that ignores payout size). A machine whose paytable doesn't match its published configuration fails both. Meter checks (hourly meters summing to daily, payouts in valid increments, bets within limits) found 0 problems.

6 · Which machines are weak for their spot, and why?

After removing luck (we use theo, not actual win), downtime (per available hour) and days off the floor, we compare each machine with the other machines in its zone. Replacement value = what an average machine in that zone would have earned per day minus what this one earned (theo). Verdict rules are listed in Methods.

Glossary (plain English)

Coin-in
Total amount wagered on a machine.
House win / hold
What the casino kept (coin-in − payouts). Hold = house win ÷ coin-in.
Theo (expected win)
What the house should keep on average given the game's published payout rate: coin-in × (1 − RTP). It removes luck.
RTP
Return to player: the share of each dollar a game pays back over the long run (a 95% RTP game keeps 5¢ per dollar).
Luck
House win − theo. Positive = the house got lucky.
z (z-score)
How unusual a result is, in standard deviations. Within ±2 is ordinary; beyond ±3 happens by chance about 1 time in 370.
Hit rate / binomial test
Share of spins that pay anything. A binomial test checks whether the observed share fits the published probability, like checking whether a coin is fair.
95% CI / range
The range we are 95% confident contains the true value.
Available hour
An hour the machine was on the floor and working (scheduled hours minus downtime).
Occupancy
Share of available hours someone was playing the machine.
Difference-in-differences
Compares the change in an affected group with the change in an unaffected group over the same dates, so floor-wide swings cancel out.
MAE
Mean absolute error: the average size of a forecast miss, in dollars per day.
Seasonal-naive forecast
"Same weekday as last week": a simple forecast any model should beat.
Lorenz curve / Gini
How concentrated spending is. The curve shows what share of wagers the smallest x% of players place; Gini 0 = everyone equal, 1 = one player does everything.
Carded play
Play by guests using a loyalty card; the only play we can see per person.

Methods, definitions and limitations

The Gilded Ace is fictional and all casino data is synthetic (TAMU Datathon "Dashboard Royale", data release 0.9.0-draft). Real public context data (LVCVA, NOAA, Missouri Gaming Commission) is used as context only. Every number on this page is reproduced by run_all.py from the raw data; see Methods for the script behind each chart.
